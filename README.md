## Overview
StudyMate AI is an AI-powered student task planning agent built using n8n and OpenAI. It helps students organize their academic tasks, prioritize deadlines, and receive personalized study plans through email.

## Problem Statement
Students often struggle to manage multiple assignments, examinations, and deadlines. StudyMate AI aims to make study planning easier by generating structured, personalized plans based on student inputs.

## Objectives
- Collect student details and academic tasks through a form.
- Generate personalized study plans using AI.
- Organize tasks according to urgency and deadlines.
- Deliver the generated study plan through email.

## Key Features
- Student task submission form.
- AI-generated study schedules.
- Task prioritization and deadline awareness.
- Email delivery of personalized study plans.
- Cloud-based workflow automation.

## Technologies Used
- n8n – Workflow automation
- OpenAI – AI-powered study plan generation
- Gmail SMTP – Email delivery
- HTML – Study plan formatting

## Workflow
1. The student submits the task form.
2. The AI node processes the student details and generates a study plan.
3. The email node sends the generated plan to the student's email address.

**Workflow:** Student Task Form → Create Study Plan → Send an Email

## How to Use
1. Open the published StudyMate AI form.
2. Enter your name, email address, tasks, deadlines, and available study hours.
3. Submit the form.
4. Check your email for the generated study plan, including the Spam folder if necessary.
